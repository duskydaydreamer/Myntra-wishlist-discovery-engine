# Myntra Slide 01 — Source Map

## [1] Bain & Company × Myntra — *E-Styling India: Decoding Online Fashion and Lifestyle Shopping Trends* (August 2024)

URL: https://preprodcms.bain.com/insights/e-styling-india-decoding-online-fashion-and-lifestyle-shopping-trends-webinar/

Claims supported:

- More than 175 million Indians shop for lifestyle products online.
- India’s current e-lifestyle market is worth approximately $16–17 billion.
- The market is projected to reach approximately $40–45 billion by 2028.
- By 2028, roughly one in five dollars spent on lifestyle is expected to be spent online.

## [2] Moneycontrol / ANI — Myntra 2025 update (December 4, 2025)

URL: https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/companies/myntra-set-to-hit-200-million-annual-active-users-in-2025-50-of-the-active-customer-base-is-gen-z-13713290.html

Claims supported:

- Myntra expected approximately 200 million annual active users by the end of 2025.
- Myntra offered more than 4 million trend-first styles.
- Repeat customers grew 25% year over year in 2025.
- Purchase frequency increased.
- Gen Z represented 50% of Myntra’s active customer base.

## [3] The Economic Times / Registrar of Companies — Myntra FY25 financials (September 12, 2025)

URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/myntras-net-profit-rises-to-rs-548-crore-in-fy25-revenue-up-18/articleshow/123855941.cms

Claims supported:

- Myntra reported ₹6,043 crore in FY25 revenue from operations.
- FY25 revenue from operations increased 18% year over year.

## [4] Mint / Datum Intelligence — Selected online-fashion market shares (2025)

URL: https://www.livemint.com/companies/nykaa-fashion-gmv-growth-profitability-11781421999258.html

Claims supported:

- Flipkart: 25.2%.
- Myntra: 17.1%.
- Amazon: 13.8%.
- AJIO: 9.0%.
- Nykaa Fashion: 2.3%.
- The source also reports Meesho at 14%; the slide intentionally displays only the five named comparison platforms and therefore does not treat the displayed values as a complete market total.

## [5] The Ken — *The rise, fall, and return of GMV* (February 10, 2024)

URL: https://the-ken.com/the-nutgraf/the-rise-fall-and-return-of-gmv/

Claim supported:

- Industry analysis from The Ken argues that maturing Indian e-commerce increased the importance of cross-selling, repeat purchases and protecting customer wallet share, while fashion e-commerce faced especially difficult growth conditions.

## [6] Graduation Project Brief

URL: Not available; supplied by the project owner.

Claim supported:

- Growth Team goal: Increase the percentage of users who purchase at least one item from their wishlist within 30 days of adding it.

## Framing notes

- The slide does not claim that Myntra has a proven wishlist problem.
- The slide does not state or estimate wishlist conversion, abandonment, users, average wishlist size, lost GMV, revenue impact or conversion uplift.
- The unresolved step between saving and purchasing is intentionally shown as a question mark because the underlying user problem has not yet been established.
